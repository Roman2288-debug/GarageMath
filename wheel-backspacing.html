Offset and backspacing describe related geometry

Offset measures from wheel centerline to the hub face in millimeters. Backspacing measures from the hub face to the innermost wheel edge in inches.

nominal backspacing = wheel width ÷ 2 + offset ÷ 25.4

Nominal wheel width is measured between bead seats, while a physical backspacing measurement reaches the wheel’s inner lip. GarageMath therefore also shows a rough lip-adjusted figure that assumes approximately one inch of total extra physical width.

Worked example

An 8-inch wheel at +25 mm offset has a nominal centerline 4 inches from the bead seat. Twenty-five millimeters is about 0.98 inch, producing about 4.98 inches of nominal backspacing. A rough lip-adjusted estimate is about 5.48 inches.

Measure the actual wheel when clearance is tight

Lay a straightedge across the inner rim lip and measure perpendicular to the hub-mounting pad. Manufacturer-published backspacing is preferred when available. The generic lip adjustment cannot know the exact flange construction of a specific wheel.

What backspacing cannot tell you

Backspacing does not prove spoke-to-caliper clearance, tire clearance, bolt pattern, hub bore, load capacity or fender fitment. Use it as one dimension in a complete fitment check.

Why the two outputs differ by half an inch

The nominal result uses bead-seat width. The lip-adjusted result assumes the physical wheel is one inch wider overall, adding half an inch on the inner side. This allowance is a generic estimate, not a measured flange dimension. Two wheels with the same advertised width and offset can have different measured lip-to-pad backspacing.

Check your measurement reference

When measuring, the straightedge sits across the inner rim lip and the depth ends at the hub-mounting face. Measuring to a spoke surface produces a different number. Record whether a seller quotes bead-seat geometry or physical backspacing before comparing that figure with this tool. Brake-spoke shape is a separate dimension and cannot be reconstructed from backspacing alone.
