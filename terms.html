Terms & Disclaimer

GarageMath calculators provide estimates for informational and planning purposes. Vehicle fitment, gearing, fuel use, engine measurements and performance can vary in the real world.

Verify critical measurements

Before purchasing parts or modifying a vehicle, verify critical dimensions, ratings, clearances and manufacturer specifications. Physical measurements should take priority when tolerances are tight.

No performance guarantee

Performance estimators are not promises of vehicle capability, safety or track results. Actual results depend on many variables not modeled by simple calculators.

Use at your own discretion

GarageMath is provided without a guarantee that every estimate will match a particular vehicle, component or operating condition.
