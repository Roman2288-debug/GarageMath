Estimates, not guarantees

Calculator outputs depend on the inputs and assumptions shown on each page. Real vehicles, components and operating conditions can differ from nominal values. Fitment, fuel-system, engine and performance results should not be treated as guarantees.

Verify critical measurements

Before purchasing parts or modifying a vehicle, verify critical dimensions, ratings, clearances and manufacturer specifications. Physical measurements and manufacturer data should take priority where tolerances are tight or safety is involved.

Performance use

Performance estimators are rough planning tools. They do not establish safe public-road speeds or guarantee track results. Performance testing should be conducted legally in a controlled environment.

GarageMath does not replace a qualified mechanic, engineer, tuner, wheel or tire specialist, inspection, alignment, dyno session or manufacturer guidance. Some decisions require expertise or measurements the website cannot provide.
