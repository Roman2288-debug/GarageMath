Why GarageMath is different

The formula is visible.

We do not ask you to trust a black box. The relevant equation and units are shown beside the tool.

The limits are visible too.

Fitment, fueling and performance have variables a simple calculator cannot know. Each tool says where the estimate stops.

Examples connect math to parts.

Worked examples show how the numbers change a wheel, tire, cruise RPM, fuel bill or engine combination.

Start with the decision, not the formula

The library connects each calculator to deeper reference material so you can understand the measurement before you spend money.

Changing tires?

Learn sidewall math, diameter changes, speedometer correction and the fitment checks the calculator cannot see.

Ordering wheels?

Understand width, offset, backspacing, inner clearance and outer position together.

Changing gearing?

See how transmission ratio, final drive and tire diameter combine to set road speed and engine RPM.
