Start with a known baseline

Use the tire code printed on the current tire and the width and offset marked on the current wheel. A catalog fitment for a similar trim is not a substitute for the hardware actually installed. The first setup is the reference for every change on this page.

Two changes can reinforce each other

Changing from an 8-inch ET40 wheel to a 9-inch ET35 wheel adds 12.7 mm to each half-width and moves the centerline outward by 5 mm. The inner wheel edge therefore consumes 7.7 mm of suspension clearance while the outer edge moves 17.7 mm toward the fender. Looking at offset alone would miss the effect of the extra width.

Changing from 225/45R17 to 245/40R18 at the same time increases nominal diameter from 24.97 to 25.72 inches. That raises geometric ride height by about 0.37 inch and increases actual speed per indicated speed by about 2.98%. Neither result tells you whether the wider tire clears the body.

What the optional measurements mean

If the current inner wheel edge has 12 mm to the suspension, subtract the 7.7 mm consumed by the example change: 4.3 mm remains in the nominal wheel-edge comparison. If the current outer edge has 15 mm to a measured fender boundary, subtract the 17.7 mm added outward: the new wheel edge passes that boundary by 2.7 mm.

A negative remaining value identifies a conflict with the measured boundary in this simplified geometry. A positive value is only a remaining dimension; it does not certify a suitable margin. These fields do not model tire section width, camber, loaded movement or brake clearance.

Take the next step with the result

  1. Check the exact tire’s approved rim-width range, load capacity and measured dimensions.
  2. Verify bolt pattern, hub bore, lug-seat type, wheel rating and brake-spoke clearance.
  3. Measure at steering lock and through suspension movement, or arrange a professional test fit.
  4. Keep the current setup, proposed setup and all measurement notes with the comparison sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Does the planner tell me if the setup fits my car?

It compares dimensions you provide. It has no vehicle database or physical scan of your car. Use it to identify what changes and what needs measurement, then verify actual fitment.

Why can the wheel clear while the tire rubs?

The tire sidewall can extend beyond the wheel edge. Its actual shape depends on the tire model, mounting width, pressure and load. Wheel-edge clearance is only one part of the problem.

Is a small diameter percentage automatically acceptable?

No. Vehicle specifications, drivetrain requirements, tire ratings and clearance still apply. This tool intentionally does not declare a universal percentage safe.

How the comparison is calculated

Wheel-edge changes use half the bead-seat width converted at exactly 25.4 mm per inch, plus or minus offset. Tire dimensions use nominal sidewall geometry. Speed scales with the ratio of new to old diameter under an unchanged calibration. The diagram compares geometry without drawing a fictional vehicle boundary.
