Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026. GarageMath provides free automotive calculators without requiring an account. Calculator values are processed by scripts in your browser and are not sent to GarageMath as form submissions.

We use Google Analytics to understand traffic and use of the site. It may process page views, approximate location, browser and device information, referral sources and interactions. We do not intentionally include your calculator entries or personally identifying information in analytics events. Google may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement. See Google's Privacy Policy and Analytics opt-out options.

GarageMath includes Google AdSense code. If ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, including ads based on visits to this or other sites where permitted. Other data and choices

If you email us, we receive the address and information you include so we can respond. You can limit cookies using your browser controls, though that may affect parts of third-party services. Our pages may link to other websites with their own privacy practices.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when site features or services change. The updated date above will reflect revisions. For privacy questions, email contact@garagemath.com.
