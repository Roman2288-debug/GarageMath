Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026. GarageMath provides free automotive calculators without requiring an account.

Calculator inputs

Calculator values are processed by scripts in your browser. GarageMath does not intentionally send the numeric values you enter as form submissions to a GarageMath database. Shareable calculator links may place the values you choose in the page URL so you can copy or share that calculation; anyone with that URL can see those values.

Analytics

GarageMath uses Google Analytics to understand traffic and product usage. Analytics may process page views, approximate location, browser and device information, referral sources and interactions such as calculator completion or result sharing. GarageMath does not intentionally include personally identifying information in custom analytics events.

Advertising

GarageMath includes Google AdSense code. If ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, limit, personalize or measure advertising where permitted. The presence of AdSense code does not necessarily mean ads are currently being displayed.

You can review Google privacy information at Google's Privacy Policy and manage ad personalization at Google My Ad Center.

Cookies and browser controls

Google services and other third-party services may use cookies or similar browser storage. You can limit or clear cookies using your browser controls, although doing so may affect third-party features.

Email contact

If you email GarageMath, the email address and information you include are received so the message can be read and answered. Do not send passwords, financial account information or other sensitive data in a correction report.

Third-party links

GarageMath links to external manufacturers, government references and other sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices and are not controlled by GarageMath.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when site features, analytics or advertising services change. Questions can be sent to contact@garagemath.com.
