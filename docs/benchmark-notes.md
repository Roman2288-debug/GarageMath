## Product decisions

- Strengthen complete tasks instead of creating near-duplicate size-specific pages.
- Preserve the established carbon/red brand and light/dark appearance.
- Pair every estimate with units, assumptions, interpretation and a next measurement.
- Use organization authorship without inventing credentials or professional reviews.
- Keep nominal geometry distinct from actual fitment; no automatic safe/unsafe percentage badges.
- Use shared formulas and regression cases rather than independent result engines.
- No change is represented as a guarantee of AdSense approval.

## Scope of validation

Formula tests and page fixtures cover eleven calculators, metric/inch tire codes, invalid inputs, optional gap budgets, alternative final drive and millimeter displacement. Structural checks cover local files, anchors, canonical metadata and sitemaps. Live UI checks are performed after the Cloudflare deployment.
