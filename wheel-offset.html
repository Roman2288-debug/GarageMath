What offset changes

Wheel offset is the distance between the wheel centerline and the mounting surface. Changing width and offset together changes both suspension-side clearance and how far the wheel sits toward the fender.

This is a geometry estimate only. Actual fitment also depends on tire width, suspension, brake clearance and bodywork.
