Compression-ratio formula

CR = (swept volume + clearance volume) ÷ clearance volume

Clearance volume includes combustion chamber volume, piston dish or dome, gasket volume and deck-clearance volume. In this tool, a piston dish is positive and a dome is negative.

Worked example

If one cylinder sweeps 500 cc and total clearance volume is 50 cc, static compression ratio is (500 + 50) ÷ 50 = 11:1. Increase clearance volume to 55 cc and the ratio falls to about 10.09:1.

Measurement quality matters

A few cubic centimeters can move the result substantially. Use measured chamber and piston volumes, actual finished bore, compressed gasket thickness and real piston-to-deck clearance when the number matters for a build.

Static is not dynamic compression

Static compression is geometric. Cam timing changes how much mixture is trapped when the intake valve closes, so dynamic compression and measured cylinder pressure can differ significantly even when static compression is unchanged.

Static compression ratio alone does not establish octane requirement or detonation margin. Combustion chamber design, cam timing, temperature, boost and tuning also matter.
