How it works

A metric tire like 225/45R17 is 225 mm wide, its sidewall is 45% of that width, and it fits a 17-inch wheel. GarageMath converts the sidewall to inches and adds two sidewalls to the wheel diameter.

Why diameter matters

A larger overall diameter travels farther per revolution. That changes the relationship between indicated speed and actual road speed, and it also changes ride height by half the diameter difference.
