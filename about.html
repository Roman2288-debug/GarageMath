About GarageMath

GarageMath makes automotive calculations easier to inspect and use. The free tools cover tire size, wheel position, gearing, engine dimensions, fuel cost and performance estimates. You can enter your own measurements without creating an account.

Our method

Each calculator uses the inputs shown on its page and runs the calculation in your browser. We show the underlying equation, a worked example and the assumptions that matter when you use the result. Results are rounded for readability; the calculation uses the entered values before display rounding.

For a tire comparison, for example, the sidewall height is section width multiplied by aspect ratio. Diameter is wheel diameter plus two sidewalls, after converting millimeters to inches. A change in diameter gives an estimated speedometer correction if the original reading was accurate. Try the tire size calculator and read the full explanation.

Where estimates end

Printed tire sizes differ from measured rolling diameter. Wheel fitment depends on tire shape, brakes, suspension and body clearance. Power and quarter-mile estimates cannot predict traction, weather or driver technique. Input quality matters as much as the formula. Check the individual tool's assumptions, then verify critical dimensions against the exact part, vehicle and manufacturer specifications before modifying a vehicle.

Corrections and scope

These tools support planning and comparisons; they do not replace a professional inspection, tune or engineering assessment. If you find an incorrect result or an unclear assumption, send the input values and expected behavior to contact@garagemath.com. We review corrections and update the explanatory content as needed.
