ABOUT GARAGEMATH

Built to make the numbers inspectable.

GarageMath is an independent automotive calculator and reference project for people comparing parts, planning changes and trying to understand the math behind a build.

Why GarageMath exists

Automotive decisions are full of repeated calculations: tire diameter, wheel position, cruise RPM, fuel cost, displacement, compression ratio, injector flow and rough performance estimates. The useful part is not only getting a number. It is knowing where that number came from, which assumptions were used and what still needs to be physically verified.

GarageMath was built around that idea. The tools are free, run in the browser and do not require an account. Major calculators include the equation, a worked example, practical interpretation, limitations and related reference material.

What GarageMath is not

GarageMath is not a substitute for a vehicle manufacturer, wheel or tire manufacturer, engine builder, tuner, inspection, alignment rack, dyno session or physical measurement. A geometric fitment calculator cannot see your brake caliper. A fuel calculator cannot measure pump voltage. A quarter-mile equation cannot know the track surface. The site is designed to make planning better, not to turn estimates into guarantees.

How content is maintained

Calculators share a common math module so formulas can be tested independently from the page design. Explanatory pages are reviewed when formulas, assumptions or source material change. Corrections can be submitted by email, and reproducible math errors are treated as bugs rather than differences of opinion.

Read How GarageMath Builds and Checks Calculators for the detailed standard.

Editorial approach

GarageMath favors practical, original explanation over keyword stuffing. We would rather publish one complete guide that explains the decision than twenty nearly identical pages built around slightly different search phrases. Sources are used where they strengthen a definition, conversion or manufacturer-specific constraint.

Contact and corrections

If you find an incorrect result, unclear explanation or useful calculator that should exist, email contact@garagemath.com. Include the page and the exact input values when reporting a calculation problem.
